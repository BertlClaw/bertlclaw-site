# Safe Claims Library

Use these phrases when evidence supports them.

## Strong, broadly safe phrasing
- clearer offer structure
- more understandable hero section
- cleaner reader flow
- easier-to-grasp service framing
- more concrete and usable website copy
- better-organized material and next steps
- deliverables that are easier to implement
- a more workable information structure
- less scattered, more decision-ready documentation

## Good evidence-backed result wording
- The offer became easier to understand within a few seconds.
- Core sections now support each other more clearly.
- The page explains the service with less ambiguity.
- The final structure reduced friction in how the offer is presented.
- Previously scattered material was turned into a usable working system.

## Risky unless specifically proven and permitted
- more leads
- higher conversion
- more revenue
- measurable growth
- proven system
- guaranteed improvement
- performance increase

## Rule
If the result is not directly visible, approved, or measured, do not phrase it as fact.
