# Internal Example Proof Atom

**Atom type:** Hero before/after  
**Status:** internal demonstrator only  
**Best reuse:** proof page, homepage proof block, proposals

## Source project
Small service-business landingpage clarification example.

## Date captured
2026-04-02

## Problem before
The opening section sounded generic and gave too little orientation. A visitor could not quickly answer three basic questions: what is this, who is it for, and what should I do next?

## Intervention
BertlClaw rewrote the hero into a clearer promise with a more specific supporting line and a more direct CTA.

## Example before
**Headline:** "Modern solutions for your business"  
**Subheadline:** "We help you grow with digital support."  
**CTA:** "Learn more"

## Example after
**Headline:** "Klare Landingpages und Website-Texte, die dein Angebot schneller verständlich machen"  
**Subheadline:** "Für kleine Unternehmen, Selbständige und Projekte, die online besser erklären müssen, was sie tun und warum man sie kontaktieren sollte."  
**CTA:** "Kontakt aufnehmen"

## Visible difference
The revised version is more specific, better targeted, and action-oriented. It reduces interpretation work and makes the next step feel more obvious.

## Why it matters
This is a compact example of BertlClaw's value: clarity is not abstract here; it becomes visible in a single screen.
