## Deliverable type
Landingpage section logic / page structure redesign.

## Source project
Small service-business landingpage clarification example.

## Short context
The business already had enough information to present its service, but the material was arranged in a way that made the page feel vague and harder to follow.

## What was delivered
A revised section order that gives the page a clearer job:
1. Problem / friction the visitor already feels
2. What BertlClaw-like service actually helps with
3. Concrete deliverables / what gets done
4. Trust / proof / reassurance
5. Clear contact step

## Why it improved the situation
The page stopped behaving like a loose collection of statements and started behaving like a guided explanation. That makes it easier for a visitor to understand the offer without extra back-and-forth.
