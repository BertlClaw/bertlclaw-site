## What it is
A practical pre-proposal execution asset for the stage between early interest and actual offer creation. It helps Dominic decide whether a lead is already proposal-ready, identify the smallest sensible starting scope, request only the missing inputs, and avoid sending vague offers too early.

## Why this matters
The workspace already had assets for outreach, reply-to-booking, discovery, post-call recap, offer templates, and post-offer follow-up. What was still missing was the exact readiness layer before proposal drafting — especially for leads that are interested but still a bit fuzzy.

## What it covers
- proposal-readiness scoring logic
- readiness stages (ready now / after short clarification / not ready)
- minimum inputs per core offer
- copy-paste input-request messages
- 10-minute scoping-call guide
- internal 90-second proposal note
- guardrails to prevent overbroad offers
