## Why it matters
The contact page already made reaching out easy, but it still left three conversion questions implicit:
1. Bin ich hier überhaupt richtig?
2. In welcher Preisregion startet das ungefähr?
3. Verschwende ich Zeit mit einer Anfrage?

This update reduces hesitation and filters for better-fit leads before submission. It should improve trust, reduce low-intent requests, and make the CTA feel safer for qualified visitors.
