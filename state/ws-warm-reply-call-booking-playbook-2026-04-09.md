## What it is
A practical reply-stage sales asset for the moment after a warm outreach message gets a response. It helps Dominic convert friendly interest, curiosity, price questions, timing hesitation, and “maybe later” replies into a concrete discovery-call booking.

## Why this was chosen
The workspace already had assets for first outreach, referral asks and handoffs, discovery call execution, quick audits, and post-call recap/follow-up. The missing layer was the exact middle step between “someone replied” and “a call is actually on the calendar”.

## What it covers
- 7 common warm-reply scenarios
- copy-paste responses for each
- concrete call-booking phrasing
- timing / price / uncertainty handling
- light referral routing
- documentation rules for reply-stage execution
