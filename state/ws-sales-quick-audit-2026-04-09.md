## What it is
A compact website quick-audit template Dominic can use before or after first contact with a prospect. It helps turn vague website feedback into a concrete, low-friction sales asset.

## Intended use
- Warm outreach after initial interest
- Pre-call or post-call written assessment
- Follow-up when a lead needs something tangible
- Referral partners who want something clear to forward

## Why this matters
Existing BertlClaw docs already covered outreach, discovery, offers, and follow-ups. What was missing was a lightweight diagnostic asset between "first message" and "formal offer". This fills that gap.
