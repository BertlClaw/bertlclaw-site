## What it is
A practical post-discovery-call execution asset for the 0–24 hours after a warm sales conversation. It gives Dominic a structured way to turn a good call into a concrete next step instead of letting momentum fade.

The workspace already had strong assets for outreach, discovery calls, follow-up sequences, referral handoffs, and pricing/offer design. What was still missing was the exact handoff layer between **"good conversation"** and **"proposal or scoped next step"**.

## What it covers
- 15-minute post-call workflow
- recap templates for different call outcomes
- proposal-progression messages
- internal post-call note template
- timing rules and quality checks
